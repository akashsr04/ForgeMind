# SOP-P201: Centrifugal Coolant Pump Troubleshooting & Cavitation

**Document ID:** DOC-P201-2024  
**Asset Type:** pump  
**Equipment:** Coolant Circulation Pump P-201 (HydraMech HM-60P)  
**Document Type:** procedure  
**Version:** 1.4  
**Source:** Plant Fluid Power Operations  

---

## 1. Overview
Pump P-201 delivers water-soluble coolant (95% water / 5% synthetic oil emulsion) to Drive Motor M-101 and CNC Station CNC-301.
- Nominal delivery rate: 45 to 60 L/min
- System operating pressure: 3.8 to 5.2 bar
- Suction strainer mesh: 100 mesh stainless steel

---

## 2. Cavitation and Flow Starvation Symptoms
- High-pitched "gravel popping" acoustic noise from the pump volute.
- Pressure oscillations exceeding +/- 1.2 bar.
- Rapid drop in delivered flow rate below 30 L/min.
- Downstream consequence: Reduced coolant flow directly causes rapid overheating in Drive Motor M-101 and CNC Spindle within 15-30 minutes.

---

## 3. Corrective Actions
1. **Suction Strainer Flush & Cleaning:**
   - Downtime: 15 minutes.
   - Isolate suction ball valve, remove basket strainer, clean accumulated particulate swarf, and re-prime pump casing.
2. **Impeller Clearance Adjustment & Seal Overhaul:**
   - Downtime: 75 minutes; Parts cost: $280.
   - Inspect mechanical shaft seal for face scoring and replace Viton O-rings.
