# SOP-C101: Belt Conveyor Tensioning, Tracking, and Drive Coupling

**Document ID:** DOC-C101-2024  
**Asset Type:** conveyor  
**Equipment:** Main Infeed Conveyor C-101 (FlexFlow FFC-800)  
**Document Type:** procedure  
**Version:** 1.2  
**Source:** Material Handling Maintenance Manual  

---

## 1. Operating Specifications
- Belt speed: 1.0 - 1.4 m/s
- Take-up tension: 8.0 - 9.2 kN
- Drive coupling: Flexible spider coupling connecting Drive Motor M-101 gearbox to head pulley.

---

## 2. Common Fault Signatures
- **Misalignment and Binding:** If belt drifts off-center, friction against lateral guide rails generates high mechanical drag, reflecting high torque back into Drive Motor M-101.
- **Drive Coupling Wear:** Worn elastomeric spider insert causes angular vibration pulses propagating into motor output shaft bearing.

---

## 3. Maintenance Interventions
1. **Pneumatic Tensioner Calibration:**
   - Downtime: 15 minutes. Check accumulator air pressure and re-tension belt to 8.5 kN.
2. **Coupling Spider Replacement:**
   - Downtime: 45 minutes; Parts cost: $120. Replace degraded polyurethane star insert.
